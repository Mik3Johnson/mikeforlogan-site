I'm a small business owner, husband and father. My wife and I came to Logan to attend Utah State University, fell in love with the valley, and have called Logan home for well over a decade.

I run [Mike Johnson Photo + Video](https://www.mikejohnsonphoto.com), a commercial photo and video company here in Logan. I've been shooting for clients since 2011 and registered the business in 2012. Running a small business has taught me to watch every dollar, listen to the people I serve, and plan for the long run. I bring the same habits to the council.

When I'm not working, you'll find me running, backpacking, rock climbing or skiing in the mountains around Cache Valley.

## On the council

Logan voters elected me to the Municipal Council in 2023. My colleagues chose me as Vice Chair in 2025 and Chair in 2026. As Chair I run our meetings and make sure residents get a real chance to be heard.

I read every agenda packet before each meeting, and I try to answer every email I get. If something in Logan matters to you, I want to hear about it.

## Why I'm running again

We've made real progress on housing and long-term planning, and there is more to do. I want to keep Logan a place where my kids, and yours, can afford to stay.
